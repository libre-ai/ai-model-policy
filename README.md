# Libre AI Model Policy

## Intended use

Help a person understand whether a proposed model use satisfies the organization’s explicitly approved rules, and which facts and conditions justify the result. An evaluation explains a policy decision; it is not itself permission to execute a model or send data to a provider.

## Existing candidates and limits

Earlier sources include TypeScript and Rust evaluation components and interface fixtures. A bounded deterministic evaluator may be reviewed separately. This documentary candidate admits no deployed approval cockpit, authenticated decision workflow, distributed package or qualified WebAssembly runtime.

## Proposed contracts

An evaluation request identifies the intended use, model facts and applicable policy revision. A result explains matched rules, unmet conditions and missing required information without turning unknown facts into approval. A policy change records its responsible authority and review. Canonical schemas and interoperability rules require admission by Contracts; proposed descriptions here do not replace them.

## Activation criteria

Pin the exact policy, model facts and evaluator revision. Demonstrate reproducible decisions and explicit refusal of missing or invalid required inputs against an independent reference. Review imported schemas, rights and dependencies. Qualify the actual interface, authentication and persistence separately from a pure evaluator, and do not treat a skipped browser or WebAssembly check as passing.

The complete journey described above is a criterion for activating the product. A candidate module may be qualified and admitted separately for its own explicit scope, with its own consumer and evidence. Neither that module admission nor the documentary existence of this repository requires a complete Missions journey or completion of other future products.
